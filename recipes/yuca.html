Yuca Fries

Description

Yuca frita is a popular Salvadoran side dish often served with fried fish, grilled meats, curtido, and tomato sauce. Crispy on the outside and tender on the inside, these yuca fries are a delicious alternative to traditional French fries.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds yuca, peeled and cut into thick strips
  • 2 quarts vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon chopped parsley (optional)
  • Curtido, for serving
  • Salvadoran tomato sauce, for serving

Steps

  1. Peel the yuca and cut it into thick fry-shaped strips.
  2. Place the yuca in a large pot and cover with water.
  3. Add a pinch of salt and boil for 20-25 minutes until the yuca is tender.
  4. Drain the yuca and allow it to cool slightly.
  5. Remove the tough fibrous core from the center of each piece.
  6. Pat the yuca dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture.
  7. Heat the vegetable oil in a deep fryer or large pot to 350°F (175°C).
  8. Fry the yuca in small batches for 4-6 minutes, or until golden and crispy.
  9. Remove the yuca with a slotted spoon and place on paper towels to drain.
  10. Mix the salt, garlic, and lime juice in a small bowl.
  11. Season the hot yuca fries with the garlic-lime mixture.
  12. Sprinkle with chopped parsley if desired.
  13. Serve immediately with curtido and Salvadoran tomato sauce.
