Salvadoran Chicken Tamales

Description

Salvadoran tamales are a traditional dish made with seasoned corn masa colored with achiote and filled with chicken, potatoes, carrots, and a flavorful tomato-based sauce. Wrapped in banana leaves and steamed until tender, they are a popular comfort food often enjoyed during holidays, family gatherings, and special celebrations throughout El Salvador.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups masa harina
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons achiote paste
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 pounds cooked chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 12 green olives (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon capers (optional)
  • 12 banana leaves, cleaned and softened

Steps

  1. Wash the banana leaves and pass them briefly over a flame or hot griddle to soften them.
  2. Cook the potatoes and carrots in boiling water until slightly tender. Drain and set aside.
  3. Blend the tomatoes, onion, bell pepper, and garlic until smooth.
  4. Cook the blended vegetables in a saucepan for 10 minutes to create a flavorful sauce.
  5. Add the achiote paste and mix well to develop the traditional Salvadoran color and flavor.
  6. In a large pot, combine the masa harina, chicken broth, oil, and salt. Cook over medium heat while stirring constantly until a smooth masa forms.
  7. Allow the masa to cool slightly before assembling the tamales.
  8. Place a softened banana leaf on a flat surface.
  9. Spread about 1/2 cup of masa in the center of the leaf.
  10. Add a piece of chicken, a few potato cubes, carrot slices, and a spoonful of the sauce.
  11. Add an olive and a few capers if desired.
  12. Fold the banana leaf securely around the filling and tie with kitchen string if needed.
  13. Repeat with the remaining ingredients.
  14. Arrange the tamales upright in a large steamer.
  15. Steam for 1 1/2 to 2 hours, adding water as necessary.
  16. Allow the tamales to rest for 10 minutes before serving.
